Maintainership is earned through sustained, high-quality contributions: reviews, issue triage, and features that land cleanly. If that describes you after a few months of activity, open an issue and ask.

Changes to the minting path, the policy engine, or the authenticator need a maintainer review regardless of who wrote them. Those three are where a subtle change turns a broker into a way around the policy engine.

Security reports go through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/ethan-kane-ops/cellcast/security/advisories/new), never public issues. See [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
