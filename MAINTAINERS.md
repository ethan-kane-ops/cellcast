| Maintainer | GitHub |
|---|---|
| Ethan Kane | [@ethan-kane-ops](https://github.com/ethan-kane-ops) |

Changes to the minting path, the policy engine, or the authenticator need a maintainer review regardless of who wrote them. Those three are where a subtle change turns a broker into a route around the policy engine.

Maintainership follows from sustained contribution: reviews, triage, and features that land cleanly. Open an issue to ask.

Security reports go through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/ethan-kane-ops/cellcast/security/advisories/new), never public issues. See [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).
